This policy explains how Freestyle Polo (the “game”, “we” or “our”) handles information when you play, view advertisements, make purchases or contact us.

At a glance
  • Core polo matches work offline and do not require a game account.
  • Your chosen rider name, settings, coins, equipment and career are saved on your device.
  • Connected release builds use GameAnalytics to understand gameplay and improve the game.
  • Google AdMob provides ads; Google Play processes Android purchases.
  • For privacy enquiries or requests, email ektishaf@gmail.com.

1. Local game information

The game saves your player name, selected options, tutorial progress, coin balance, owned mallets, horse-training levels, match-entry state, purchase-credit identifiers and local career history on your device. Career history can include your chosen name, teams, stadium, scores, rewards and match date.

These saves allow the game to restore progress, apply purchases and avoid crediting the same purchase twice. We do not operate a game-account system or our own cloud-save service. Device or platform backup features may separately copy app data according to your device settings. Do not use sensitive personal information as your player name.

2. Gameplay analytics

Release builds initialize GameAnalytics automatically when configured. There is currently no in-game analytics enable/disable switch. Analytics helps us understand tutorial completion, match starts and results, stadium and difficulty choices, goals, player switching, coin earnings and spending, equipment upgrades, purchase outcomes and advertising delivery.

GameAnalytics may process a pseudonymous installation or device identifier, session and event timestamps, IP/network information, country or approximate location derived from IP, device and operating-system information, and app/build version. The exact SDK metadata depends on the platform and provider configuration. Pseudonymous information can still be personal data.

Our custom gameplay events do not include your chosen player name, email address, payment-card details, purchase receipt contents, purchase tokens or local match identifiers. This does not mean that analytics providers collect no device information. Offline events may be stored by the SDK and sent after connectivity returns.

See GameAnalytics privacy information and its data-processing information. Ad Privacy Choices manages advertising preferences; it does not switch off GameAnalytics.

3. Advertising and Ad Privacy Choices

Google AdMob supplies menu banners, interstitial ads at match transitions, and optional rewarded videos. Advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app/device information, ad requests, impressions, interactions and fraud-prevention information. Processing and ad eligibility depend on your region, device settings, consent choices and the provider's configuration.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays privacy messages. When Google requires a way to revisit them, Settings shows Ad Privacy Choices. Depending on the form and region, you may review advertising partners and purposes, change consent for personalization or device storage/access, withdraw previously given consent, or exercise applicable advertising opt-outs.

These choices are not an ad-free purchase or a general “remove ads” switch. Your choices may lead to personalized, non-personalized or more limited advertising, or affect ad availability. Declining personalization does not guarantee that every ad request can still be served. Rewarded-video coins are granted only when the provider reports that the reward was earned.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's ad-serving modes. You may also use your device's advertising privacy controls.

4. In-app purchases

Android coin purchases are processed by Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing. Google handles payment and account information under its own policies. The game receives product and transaction/receipt information to validate purchases, deliver coins, recover pending transactions and prevent duplicate credits. Purchase identifiers and credited-transaction records are retained in the local save.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number in the game. Custom analytics records purchase attempts/outcomes and awarded coin amounts, rather than receipt contents or purchase tokens. Store purchase records and refunds remain subject to the store's processes. See Google privacy information and Unity's game-player privacy information.

5. Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue and handle privacy requests. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Providers and other disclosures

We use GameAnalytics for analytics, Google AdMob/UMP for advertising and privacy messaging, Google Play for purchases/distribution, and Unity technology to run and support the game. These providers process information for the functions described above under their applicable terms and policies. Advertising-related processing may be subject to additional regional choices.

We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal request, protect users or prevent fraud. We do not publish your local career history or player name as a public leaderboard.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game saves remain until removed through device/app-storage controls, uninstalling the game, or other platform behaviour. Clearing data can permanently remove coins, equipment, settings and career progress; the game does not provide cloud recovery of a consumed coin balance. Device backups and store transaction records are controlled separately by the relevant platform.

Provider-side analytics and advertising information is retained under the provider's applicable retention rules and account configuration. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary records or meet legal obligations, and remove information when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

To request access or deletion of information processed through the game, contact us. We may need limited information to verify and locate the relevant records. We will explain if a record cannot be located, must be retained, or is controlled by a provider that handles the request directly. Uninstalling does not itself delete information already held by providers or store purchase records.

8. Your rights and controls

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us using the address below to make a request. Applicable rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction.

Ad Privacy Choices and device advertising settings control supported advertising preferences. They do not delete local saves or control independent analytics. This policy explains current processing; it does not replace any notice or consent required by applicable law.

9. Children

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the game or support communications, contact us so we can investigate and address the request. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and use the store's parental controls where appropriate.

10. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information involved, but no app, local save, network transmission or storage system is completely secure. Providers may process data outside your country under their applicable transfer arrangements and safeguards. Consult their policies for details.

11. This policy website

This static page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics script, advertising, contact form or cookies added by us. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to provider websites have their own privacy practices.

12. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. Updates will be published here with a revised date; additional notice will be provided where required.
